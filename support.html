Frequently Asked Questions

How do I block my lost card?

You can instantly block your card via the 'Card Protection' section on our app or website.

How to increase my credit limit?

Visit the 'Management' section and select 'Increase Limit' to request an upgrade based on your eligibility.

How can I redeem reward points?

Go to the 'Rewards' section, browse the catalog, and click 'Redeem' to use your points.
